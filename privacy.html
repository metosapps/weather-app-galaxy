Information used by the app

  • Location and places. With Android location permission, the app can use approximate or precise coordinates to obtain local weather. Places you search for or save, location names, coordinates, country and time-zone information can be stored on your device and sent to weather, search, geocoding, air-quality and radar services to provide the requested information. A network-based lookup may estimate a location from your IP address. Widgets and scheduled weather updates can use saved locations.
  • Local preferences. The app stores settings such as units, language, selected locations, display preferences, notification settings, cached forecasts, consent choices and Premium status. Android app storage controls can remove these local records.
  • App and device information. Integrated Google/Firebase services can process installation and anonymous authentication identifiers, app version, device and operating-system details, language, country, interaction events, crashes, performance measurements and network request information. These support app operation, configuration, reliability and usage measurement. An anonymous service identifier is still an identifier; it is not a named profile or proof that no data is collected.
  • Notifications. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a device registration token. The app can send that token, notification enabled/disabled preferences, scheduled notification times, time zone, app version and Android version to Firebase so weather notifications can be delivered. Registration may also record that notifications are disabled. Notification permission and weather-notification settings control what is displayed on your device.
  • Advertising. When ads are requested, Google Mobile Ads can process IP address, approximate location derived from it, advertising or app identifiers where available, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Available ad formats include banner, native, interstitial and app-open ads. Test builds use Google test ad units; test ads still use the Google SDK and its network services.
  • Feedback and reports. If you use an in-app feedback or weather-report feature, the text, contact information and attachments you choose to submit, together with relevant weather, location, app or device context, can be uploaded to Firebase Firestore or Cloud Storage. Please avoid sensitive information and files you do not want to share. Email support receives your email address, message and any attachments you send.
  • Purchases. In versions with Samsung in-app purchases, Samsung handles checkout and account/payment processing. The app receives product identifiers, purchase identifiers, purchase and subscription status, and relevant dates to acknowledge, restore and manage Premium access. It keeps a local entitlement cache. It does not receive your full card number or Samsung account password. Earlier app versions may use a different store billing provider; manage those purchases with the store that processed them.

Services that receive information

Weather and location features connect to services hosted at swtapi.tohapp.com, airapi.tohapp.com and sradar.tohapp.com, and may use ipfind.co for network-based location. Weather/search requests may be relayed to upstream weather or geocoding services. These services receive the requested place or coordinates and normal connection information, including IP address. Availability and upstream data sources may change. Embedded radar pages enable scripts and local site storage and can load provider resources. Their data handling is separate from the static website described below.

Google/Firebase provides advertising, analytics, crash and performance reporting, anonymous authentication, messaging, cloud data storage and remote configuration. Samsung provides Galaxy Store distribution and supported in-app purchases. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy and information from partner apps
  • Firebase privacy and security
  • Samsung Privacy Policy — use the Samsung privacy notice for your country where applicable

This website and remote configuration

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Updated app versions also retrieve a small public settings file from GitHub to control supported ad placements and links. Those requests contain normal connection metadata such as IP address and user-agent information; our configuration request does not add your location, purchase receipt, advertising ID or email address to the URL or request body. GitHub may keep service logs under its Privacy Statement.

We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies to this website. Its images and styles are served with the site. Clicking Galaxy Store or a third-party policy link takes you to that provider. Clicking an email link opens your email application; nothing is sent automatically.

Your choices

  • You can refuse or revoke location permission in Android settings and use manually selected places where supported. Some automatic local-weather features may then be unavailable.
  • You can change notification settings in the app and in Android. Turning off notifications does not itself delete previously stored cloud records.
  • Where offered, the advertising consent form lets you make regional privacy choices. Updated versions provide an Ad privacy choices entry to reopen the available privacy form. Android also provides advertising-ID controls. Ad consent choices do not automatically disable every independent Firebase service.
  • Premium ad removal, when purchased and verified, controls advertising in the app. It does not stop the weather and purchase checks needed for those features.
  • You can remove saved places and clear app storage or uninstall the app. This removes local data; it does not automatically cancel subscriptions or delete records already held by service providers.

Retention, security and international processing

Local data remains until replaced or removed through app or Android controls. Support messages, submitted reports and cloud records are retained as needed to provide support and services, investigate problems, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Provider retention periods differ; there is no single retention period for all app data. Payment providers may retain transaction records for their own legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion of records that can be identified and are within the publisher’s control.

We use available platform storage protections and HTTPS service connections. No device, network or storage system is completely secure. Providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Their notices explain their processing locations and transfer safeguards.
